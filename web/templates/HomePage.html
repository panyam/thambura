{{ brand }} keeps the tala and plays the drone while you practise. It is free, runs in your browser, and needs no account or install.

Tala keeper

All seven sapta talas (Eka, Rupakam, Matya, Jhumpa, Thriputa, Ata and Dhruva) in any of the five jaathis, the chaapu talas (Thisra, Khanda, Misra, Viloma and Sankeerna) and Adi. Pick the nadai and kalai, and any tempo from 10 to 300 bpm. Each beat shows its kriya on a right or left hand, the clap, the finger count or the wave, and the hand lifts and drops with the beat as it does when you keep thalam.

Thambura drone

The thambura (tambura or tanpura) plucks its four strings, the first on Pa, Ma, Ni or any swarasthana, then Sa, Sa and low Sa, at any shruthi from A to B (each key also shows its kattai number, C being 1). Its main voice was fitted to a recording of a real tambura, and there is also a classic synthesized tambura, a guitar-like pluck and a shruthi box reed drone. Play it on its own or with the tala.
